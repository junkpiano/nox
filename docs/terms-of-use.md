## What nox is

nox is a client for the Nostr protocol. It is a tool for reading from and
writing to relays that you choose. It is not a service, and there is no account
to hold: the developer operates no server, stores none of your data, and cannot
see, moderate, or remove anything you or anyone else publishes.

nox is provided free of charge, as is, with no warranty of any kind. The
developer is not liable for any loss arising from its use.

## Your key is your identity

Your Nostr private key is the only proof that an account is yours. It is stored
on your device and nowhere else.

**If you lose it, it cannot be recovered.** Not by the developer, not by
anybody. Uninstalling the app, resetting the device, or clearing the credential
store destroys the key it holds. Keep your own backup.

Anyone who obtains your key controls your account permanently.

## Your wallet is your own

Connecting a Lightning wallet stores a permission to that wallet on this device.
The developer never holds, sees, or can spend your funds, and takes no part in
any payment. Payments made over Lightning are irreversible. You are responsible
for what you send.

**There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour.**
You agree not to use nox to publish content that is unlawful, that harasses,

You are solely responsible for everything you publish. Because Nostr is public
and decentralised, what you publish may be copied and retained by relays and by
other people, and cannot reliably be unpublished.

## Reporting and blocking

nox provides the tools a decentralised network allows:

- **Mute** an account, from its profile or from any of its posts, to stop seeing
  its posts, replies and notifications. Muted accounts are listed in Settings
  and can be unmuted there.
- **Report** a post or an account, with a reason. Reports are published to
  relays for relay operators and moderators to act on.

Reports are reviewed by the operators of the relays involved, not by the
developer of nox, who has no ability to remove content from a network they do
not run. **Where the developer is notified of objectionable content and does
have the ability to act, they will do so within 24 hours** — including removing
a relay from the app's defaults, and if necessary blocking the account within
the app.

If content is unlawful, report it to the relevant relay operator or to the
appropriate authorities.
