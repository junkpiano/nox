What it does

  • Your timeline, people and hashtags. Follow people and hashtags; new posts wait at the top until you want them.
  • Private messages. End-to-end encrypted, with the relays you choose for them.
  • Zaps and a Lightning wallet. Connect a wallet over Nostr Wallet Connect, or pay any invoice by QR code.
  • Reactions with any emoji. Including custom emoji, and a one-tap reaction you choose.
  • Your key stays yours. Kept in the system keychain on the apps, or never handed over at all with a browser extension or remote signer.
